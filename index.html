What it is

It is plain text. That is all, like a note. You copy it, paste it into your assistant, and your assistant reads it and does the work. No downloads.

How to start

Setup takes under 10 minutes. Say yes or no to each choice. That is the whole setup.

  1. Press Copy it. You can press Read it first to see every line before you paste.
  2. Paste it into your assistant. Start a new chat in Claude or a similar assistant, paste, and say "Set up my dashboard."
  3. Get your setup rating from 1 to 100, a list of free connectors that raise it, and a dashboard you can bookmark.

Automated or manual setup

With automated setup, your assistant does the work and you say yes or no to each choice. With manual setup, you pick every step yourself. Most people should pick automated. Either way you can say no to anything.

What you get

Your assistant builds one page with these tabs, filled only from sources it can reach. A tab with nothing connected shows one line saying what to connect.

Today

The date, the last refresh time, the weather if reachable, and a ranked to-do list of the 5 to 10 most important things.

Agenda

Day, week, and month views of every connected calendar in one place, with conflicts marked.

Inbox

Messages grouped into needs a reply, waiting on others, and for your information, with drafted replies in your voice. Nothing is sent without your yes.

Messages and family

Unread chat messages and mentions, plus the people you named, with upcoming birthdays and a button to draft a message to each.

Money

If connected read-only: this month's spending, subscriptions, and anything unusual. It never moves money.

Have fun

Ideas matched to what you enjoy, leaving out what you never want suggested.

My AI Journey

Where you stand with AI and what to try next. It compares you to five rough profiles, from a grandparent new to AI to a software engineer. It is a guide, not a grade.

Your setup rating

Email 15 points, calendar 15, tasks and notes 10, team chat 10, files 10, scheduled refresh 10, money read-only 10, fun and media 5, travel 5, home 5, and web search 5. The total is your rating, from 1 to 100.

Safe and private

The text is plain. Press Read it first and read every line. It contains no code that runs by itself, and it sends nothing anywhere.

  • The person is in charge. Your assistant asks before it sends, posts, buys, deletes, accepts, or changes anything.
  • Work and personal data stay apart behind a privacy firewall.
  • Nothing leaves. Your data stays between you and your assistant, and nothing is sent to claudebomb.com.
  • Your assistant is truthful. If it cannot reach something, it says so, and it never invents emails, events, balances, or numbers.
  • It never asks for passwords or codes.
