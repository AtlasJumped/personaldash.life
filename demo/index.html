Well-being check Optional

Everyone can rate everyone, themselves included, so no one feels singled out. Healthy traits are here too.

Tap a zone. It looks like a slider, but each zone is a button. 3 in the middle is about average for most people. 1 is exceptionally strong. 5 is a real struggle.

Use this sparingly. Blind spots make the picture less useful. You can still skip any you want.

This is not a medical tool or a diagnosis, and it is not accepted medical practice. It is a way for a family to talk. If anyone may be unsafe, talk in person, soon and calmly, and get professional help.
